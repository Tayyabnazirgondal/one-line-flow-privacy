Privacy Policy

One Line Flow — Daynix Studio

Effective Date: September 2026

Short version: One Line Flow is a fully offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing. One Line Flow does not request, collect, transmit, or store any personal data, identifiers, or usage analytics.

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or location is collected.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are integrated in the app.
  • No cookies or web beacons are used.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

The app saves the following information locally on your device only so that your progress is not lost between sessions:

  • Level progress, stars earned, and best times.
  • Daily puzzle streak and achievement unlocks.
  • Your preferences (theme, colorblind mode, haptic feedback, handedness).

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server operated by us. If you uninstall the app or use the built-in "Reset Progress" option, this data is permanently deleted.

3. Permissions

One Line Flow does not request any sensitive permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, location, or any other personal information on your device.

4. Internet Access

The app works fully offline. No internet connection is required to play, and the app does not make any network requests during normal use.

5. Third-Party Services

One Line Flow does not integrate any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics providers
  • Social media SDKs
  • Cloud storage or authentication providers

6. Children's Privacy

One Line Flow is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or applicable regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored outside your device, there is no user data on our side to access, modify, or delete. You have full control over all app data through your device settings and the in-app "Reset Progress" option.
